### p4-seed-config-dotted-keys · discovery · seed_config.py was top-level-only — the planned quality.lint.enabled invocation would have seeded a dead flat key; dotted-key nested seeding implemented (phase 4)

Top-level flat keys (voice_level) behave exactly as before. seed_config.py has NO template mirror — no re-copy needed.
